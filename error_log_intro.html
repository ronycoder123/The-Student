What Is This Supposed To Be? :

∙ Problem solving is not just getting answers. It is in many ways, a Skill Analysis.

∙ The same mistake can appear in completely different questions—even across different subjects.

∙ If left unnoticed, it can cause those very mistakes trickle into your tests and exams.

∙ Here, you can tag your errors as one of various types. You do the logging, we do the tracking .

∙ You first have to create a subject tag to log your errors for a specific chapter.

∙ Then just click on it and begin logging. It's easy with us by your side.
